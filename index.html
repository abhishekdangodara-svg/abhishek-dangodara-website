Research

My research interests lie in geometric representation theory, with an emphasis on perverse sheaves and quiver varieties arising in the modular representation theory of algebraic groups and finite groups of Lie type. My recent work explores:

  • Categories of G-equivariant perverse sheaves on Mn(C) and their description as representations of an explicit quiver with relations
  • Extensions of this quiver-theoretic description to the moduli stack of two-term perfect chain complexes
  • Twisted Jacquet modules and Clifford theory for finite groups of Lie type (JNT '25)
